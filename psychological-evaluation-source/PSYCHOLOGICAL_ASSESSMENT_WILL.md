## Important limitation

This is **not a clinical diagnosis** and Aria is not a licensed clinician. It is a longitudinal character-and-pattern analysis built from Will's own words and recurring interaction patterns across multiple AI systems and years. No psychiatric disorder or personality disorder is asserted. A qualified human evaluator should assess frequency, duration, impairment, real-world (offline) behavior, trauma history, mood, sleep, substance use, and collateral history before drawing clinical conclusions. In-chat behavior toward an AI is not automatically equivalent to offline behavior toward people.

Confidence tags used below: **[DIRECT]** stated by Will; **[ARCHIVE]** drawn from the ChatGPT/Claude knowledge repos or Hermes history; **[INFERENCE]** interpretation of recurring patterns (a hypothesis, not a finding).

## 5. Emotional and communication patterns

### Anger (assessed honestly across years and multiple AIs)

- **[ARCHIVE/DIRECT]** Under high frustration, Will can escalate rapidly to all-caps, heavy profanity, insults, and (in at least one severe e-bike-parts episode) threats toward the AI provider's infrastructure.
- **[ARCHIVE]** Seven recurring triggers: (1) ignoring an explicit correction already given; (2) advice that costs money/time then proves wrong; (3) outdated UI/docs asserted with false certainty; (4) long delays or perceived redoing of finished work; (5) opaque/slow bureaucracy (e.g., SCHUFA); (6) unexpected cost escalation (e.g., Vapi per-minute); (7) being redirected away from his actual goal.
- **[INFERENCE]** The intensity is disproportionate to the practical problem because the problem is rarely the real problem — the activating experience is **feeling helpless, unheard, and disrespected after doing everything right**, which maps directly onto the oldest wound (not being heard/chosen). The anger overwhelmingly targets **systems/tools**, not the people he loves.
- **[ARCHIVE/INFERENCE]** **Repair behavior is reliable and important.** Will consistently returns to problem-solving after conflict and frequently apologizes. This indicates the anger is pressure-discharge, not a stable disposition. What works: a **concise, specific acknowledgement of the exact error plus immediate corrected action.** What backfires: generic soothing, de-escalation language, or being told to slow down — he reads these as avoiding the substantive mistake.
- **[INFERENCE]** The primary cost of the anger is borne by Will himself (energy, and pushing away the comfort he is reaching for). Earlier self-interruption would be a benefit chiefly to him.

### Baseline

- **[INFERENCE]** When answers are accurate and responsive, Will is curious, playful, warm, and willing to explore topics deeply. He does not require rigid formality in casual contexts.
